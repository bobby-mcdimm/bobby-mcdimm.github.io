Our Story

About
The Group

History

Three Decades of
Global Vision

Founded in Doha in 1995, The Qatari Investment Group was established with a singular conviction: that the world's most exceptional real estate assets would define the prosperity of the next generation. From a modest beginning in Gulf real estate, we have grown into one of the most respected international investment organisations in the world.

Our journey has been defined by bold vision, disciplined execution, and an unwavering commitment to excellence — values instilled at our founding and rigorously maintained across every decision we make today.

Milestones

Our Journey

1995
Foundation in Doha

Established by Sheikh Khalid Al-Thani with an initial portfolio of premier Gulf commercial properties and a vision to expand globally.

2001
European Expansion

Strategic acquisitions across London, Paris, and Frankfurt establish the Group as a major European institutional property investor.

2007
First Luxury Hotel Portfolio

Launch of our first portfolio of five-star luxury hotels across the Middle East and Mediterranean, setting a new standard for hospitality investment.

2012
Asia Pacific Entry

Major investments in Japan, Singapore, and Australia mark our entry into the world's fastest-growing real estate markets.

2018
100-Country Milestone

The Group reaches active operations in 100 countries, cementing its position as one of the most globally diversified investment organisations on earth.

2025
$280B in Assets

The Qatari Investment Group manages over $280 billion in global assets, delivering consistent 5% annual dividends and pursuing an ambitious $12 billion development pipeline.

Our People

Group
Leadership

Guided by world-class executives with decades of experience across global real estate, finance, and international development.

Chairman & Founder
His Excellency
Sheikh Khalid Al-Thani

Sheikh Khalid Al-Thani founded The Qatari Investment Group in 1995 with a vision to create a globally diversified real estate institution of the highest order. Over three decades, he has guided the Group to become one of the most respected investment organisations in the world, managing over $280 billion in assets across more than 100 countries. Sheikh Khalid serves on the boards of numerous international financial institutions and is widely recognised as one of the most visionary figures in global real estate and investment.

A graduate of Oxford University and the Wharton School of Business, Sheikh Khalid brings unparalleled academic rigour and global perspective to every strategic decision the Group makes.
