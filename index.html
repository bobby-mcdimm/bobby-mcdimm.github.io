Foundation

A New Kind of
Capital House

Founded in 2008 in Doha, Qatar, QIG was established to channel sovereign-grade capital discipline into a globally diversified real estate and infrastructure platform. Unlike conventional funds, QIG operates with permanent capital — allowing our investment teams to act decisively across market cycles.


Today, QIG manages $48 billion in assets across 100+ countries, with active developments spanning luxury residential, ultra-prime commercial, world-class hospitality, and strategic infrastructure. Our institutional DNA — forged in partnership with Gulf sovereign entities — informs every decision we make.
